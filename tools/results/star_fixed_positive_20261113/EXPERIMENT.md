# Fixed-book positive-only control

This fresh audit retains the 33/36 version's complete book and tests the new
Rapfi positive-only policy. No training occurs while these games run. Empirical
bans, negative ordering penalties and loss-triggered PV expansion are disabled
for Rapfi, and the experimental Black hole-risk heuristic is off. Alpha's book
and branch-aware routing are retained. The equal-depth comparison fix remains.

The old 33/36 score is not reused. Only these 36 new games can pass this audit.
Parallel opening trials are training-only data and cannot contribute passes.
See LIVE_REPORT.md and summary.json as games complete.
